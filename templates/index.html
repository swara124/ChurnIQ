How it works

Three simple steps from raw data to actionable churn predictions.

Step 01

Upload Your Data

Upload any bank customer CSV file. The system auto-detects columns, cleans missing values, and encodes categorical fields — no manual prep needed.

Step 02

Feature Engineering

15+ derived features are built automatically — balance ratios, loyalty flags, credit bands, engagement scores — then ranked by mutual information scoring.

Step 03

Prediction & Report

Three models compete and the best-performing ensemble labels each customer with a risk percentage and churn reasons you can act on immediately.
